Here are some datasheet for Pixart IAS sensors.
- PAW3805EK: this model can track move on glass. The CJV1 variant use Infra-Red LED (full 29 pages and brief 12 pages), while CJV2 use blue LED (medium 20 pages without register list).
- PMW3325: older model (brief 19 pages).
- PMW3360: a popular model with high CPI, build-in accumulate burst (full 57 pages).
- PMW3389: a high-end gaming sensor with high CPI (brief 20 pages).
- PMW3610: a popular model with lower CPI and low-power consumption. The datasheet has all details (full 38 pages).
