The Ball Socket is built on a 180° semi-sphere.

Due to production error, the purchased billiard cue ball is 56.4mm (R=28.2) in diameter (instead of standard 57.2mm).

### Socket Shell
- ID: 59mm (IR 29.5), OD: 66mm (OR 33).
- Wall: 3.5mm.

### Bearing Holes
- Bearing ball diameter: 3mm.
- Three bearings positioned at 22.5°S, 120° apart.
- Bearing hole has a 0.3mm extruding from inside the Socket Shell.
- The Hole is 3mm diameter (printing modelled 3.1mm for 3D printing shrinkage compensation), with cone bottom of 0.866mm height.
- Below the Hole, there's 1.2mm diameter pin hole to remove the bearing ball in case.
- The whole Bearing Hole structure is 3.8mm in height, burying 2mm of bearing ball, with 1mm exposure.
- The designed diameter of Bearing Hole top is 58.4mm (59 - 0.3*2; R=29.2).
- With 1mm exposure of ball bearing, ideally support exact 56.4mm diameter ball.

### Sensor Holder
- Sensor board size: 24.7 x 17.30mm; lens to ball surface: 2.40mm.
- Lens head: 14 x 9 x 1mm; lens neck: 11.5 x 6 x 3mm.
- Optical window: 5 x 8mm.
- Designed diameter of lens surface is 61.2mm (56.4 + 2.4*2; R=30.6), 2.4mm (33 - 30.6) from outer Shell.
